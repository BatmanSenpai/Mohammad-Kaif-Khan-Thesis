Lipoprotein(a) and Family History of CVD as Independent Cardiovascular Risk Pathways: The Modifying Role of Lifestyle on Pediatric Blood Pressure — Evidence from the Hand aufs Herz Study

Mohammad Kaif Khan | August 2026 | Master's Thesis, IBE, LMU München

What this code does: Cross-sectional secondary analysis of the Hand aufs Herz (H&H) Study. Tests whether Lp(a) concentration and family history of CVD are associated with blood pressure, and whether a composite lifestyle score modifies either association. Primary method: multivariable linear regression adjusted for age, sex, and BMI z-score.

How to run: 

Place df.csv in the project root 
Open Kaif_Thesis.Rmd in RStudio
Install dependencies: dplyr, ggplot2, gtsummary, broom, MASS, nnet, mediation, flextable, officer, car, GGally, DiagrammeR

Click Knit — outputs go to Kaif_Thesis.pdf, manuscript_full.docx, manuscript_tables.docx, and figures.doc
